The pipeline

Four steps, drawn extent to vectors

01

Draw AOI

Outline a field extent on the satellite map. The drawn shape is the only input — no preset bounds.

02

Fetch imagery

Mosaic the covering XYZ tiles into a georeferenced GeoTIFF. Zoom 18–19 resolves field edges.

03

Segment

SAM 2 automatic masks label every parcel. points_per_side and IoU tune granularity for small fields.

04

Vectorize

Masks become polygons, then boundary lines. Sliver filter and Douglas–Peucker clean the geometry; export GeoJSON.

Validated

The vector step, proven

STEP 04 · reproducible offline

Tile download and SAM inference need internet and a GPU, so they run in Colab. The geometry that turns a raster mask into boundary lines does not.

Here it runs on a synthetic 40-parcel mask georeferenced to the Bhola AOI, producing 40 clean LineString boundaries. This validates the vectorize step on its own — it is not a segmentation of the scene in the hero.

Field notes

What to watch

GPU is not optional

SAM 2 on CPU is minutes per tile. Cache the model once and run inference on a GPU backend.

Mind the imagery licence

Google tiles are for prototyping. For Bangladesh prefer Esri World Imagery or a licensed feed — NAIP is US-only.

Smallholder fields are hard

Auto-SAM over- and under-segments small fragmented parcels. Tune points_per_side, filter by area, treat output as a first draft.
